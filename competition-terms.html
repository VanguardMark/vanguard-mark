OFFICIAL COMPETITION RULES

Competition Terms & Conditions

These terms explain how the Vanguard Mark logo competition operates and what participants must know before entering.

1. Agreement

By creating an account, submitting a logo, or entering the Vanguard Mark competition, you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, do not participate.

2. Competition Period

The competition runs during the officially announced entry period. Entries must be successfully submitted before the published closing deadline. Times are determined by the official competition announcement.

Late or incomplete submissions may be excluded unless an extension is officially announced.

3. Eligibility

The competition is intended to welcome eligible participants without a general country-based restriction, subject to applicable laws, sanctions, and payment-provider requirements.

Participants must be at least 18 years old and legally eligible to enter. Each participant is responsible for ensuring their participation is lawful where they live.

4. Entry Fees

The entry fee is US$3 per logo submission. Each separate logo submission requires its own entry fee.

Payment does not guarantee selection, a favourable judging result, or a prize. Participants should retain their transaction confirmation.

Refund requests are handled under the Refund Policy.

5. Submission Requirements

Submitted designs must be original work or work the participant has permission to enter.

  • Do not copy or unlawfully use another person's protected work.
  • Do not submit another person's design as your own.
  • Do not include unlawful or deliberately deceptive material.
  • Do not upload malicious files or interfere with the website.
  • Follow the file requirements shown on the submission page.

Participants are responsible for ensuring their submissions are complete, accessible, and legally suitable for entry.

6. Ownership and Use of Designs

Participants retain the intellectual property rights they own in their designs unless those rights are transferred through a separate lawful written agreement.

By submitting a logo, participants give Vanguard Mark limited, non-exclusive permission to store, reproduce, display internally, and share the submission with authorised judges and administrators as needed to operate, evaluate, verify, and document the competition.

This permission does not itself transfer ownership or grant unrestricted commercial use of a design. Any additional rights requested from a winner must be addressed separately where applicable.

7. Judging

Experienced judges will evaluate eligible submissions. Judging may consider originality, creativity, visual quality, relevance to the brief, clarity, professional execution, and practical usability.

Judges assess submissions according to the competition criteria. Paying an entry fee does not guarantee favourable treatment or selection.

Concerns about a material judging error, fraud, or a conflict of interest may be reported to support for review.

8. Grand Prize

The advertised grand prize is US$50,000. Vanguard Mark represents that the prize has been fully secured.

The prize is awarded to the eligible winner selected through the judging process, subject to verification and acceptance requirements. No additional prizes are promised unless officially announced.

The winner is responsible for applicable taxes, except where the law requires another party to withhold or pay them.

9. Winner Verification

A potential winner may be asked to verify their age, identity, eligibility, contact details, and rights to the submitted work.

Prize delivery arrangements will be communicated to the verified winner. Processing may depend on verification, payment services, and applicable legal requirements.

If a winner is ineligible, cannot be verified, declines the prize, or materially breaches these terms, the organiser may consider another eligible entry where reasonably possible.

10. Fair Participation

Participants must not manipulate the competition, submit fraudulent information, interfere with judging, access another person's account, falsify payment records, or infringe others' rights.

Attempts to compromise website security or disrupt the competition may result in investigation and appropriate action.

11. Disqualification

Vanguard Mark may investigate suspected fraud, infringement, security threats, or material breaches. Any restrictions or disqualification should be proportionate to the circumstances and subject to applicable law.

Disqualification does not remove refund rights that apply under the Refund Policy or applicable law.

12. Website Availability and Liability

Reasonable efforts will be made to maintain the website, but uninterrupted availability cannot be guaranteed. Hosting services, payment providers, internet connections, and user devices may experience failures.

Participants should keep copies of their designs and payment confirmations. Nothing in these terms excludes liability where doing so would be unlawful.

13. Changes or Cancellation

Competition procedures may be reasonably adjusted to address security issues, technical problems, misconduct, or circumstances affecting fair operation.

Material changes will be communicated through official channels. If the competition is cancelled or cannot proceed, entry payments will be handled according to the Refund Policy and applicable law.

14. Privacy

Personal information is handled according to the Privacy Policy. Participants should provide accurate information and avoid including unnecessary personal details in uploaded designs.

15. General Provisions

If a provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue to apply to the extent permitted by law. Nothing in these terms removes rights that cannot legally be excluded.

16. Contact

For questions about the competition, entries, judging, or prizes, contact Vanguard Mark Support at support@vanguardmark.com.

Do not send passwords or full payment-card details by email.
