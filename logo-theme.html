01 / The Brief

What are you being asked to create?

This is the central brief for the Vanguard Mark logo design competition. Read it before developing your concept.

The Vanguard Mark Logo Challenge

Create a distinctive logo identity for Vanguard Mark.

Design an original logo concept that expresses a forward-looking vision, confidence, leadership, progress, and creative ambition. The design should feel professional, memorable, and suitable for a brand that wants to establish a strong visual identity.

Your logo may use typography, a symbol, a monogram, geometric forms, or a combination of these elements. The specific visual approach is your decision. What matters is that the result feels intentional, recognisable, balanced, and original.

The objective: Present a logo that communicates a clear identity and can work effectively across digital platforms, printed materials, and other brand applications.

02 / Brand Direction

The qualities your design should communicate.

Use these principles to guide your creative decisions. They describe the intended character of the brand, not a requirement to use any particular symbol or illustration.

01 / VISION

Forward-Thinking

Develop a concept that suggests ambition, new possibilities, and a clear sense of direction. Avoid designs that feel generic or lack a recognisable identity.

02 / CONFIDENCE

Strong and Distinctive

Aim for a confident visual presence. Every shape, letter, and proportion should contribute to the overall identity rather than compete for attention.

03 / PRECISION

Clean and Considered

Pay attention to spacing, alignment, proportions, and legibility. A professional logo should look deliberate rather than overcrowded or unnecessarily complicated.

04 / RECOGNITION

Memorable and Original

Develop a concept with its own visual character. Avoid copying existing brands or relying on familiar symbols without giving them a distinctive purpose.

03 / Design Considerations

What makes a strong logo?

Evaluate your design against these practical considerations before submitting your entry.

01

Clear Recognition

The main idea should be understandable without requiring a long explanation. Aim for a distinctive visual identity that viewers can recognise and remember.

02

Typography and Readability

If your design includes the Vanguard Mark name or other lettering, ensure the text is legible, well-spaced, and visually consistent with the rest of the concept.

03

Versatility

Consider how the design would appear on a website, mobile screen, document, social media profile, or printed material.

04

Colour and Contrast

Choose colours deliberately. Your concept should maintain sufficient contrast and should not depend entirely on special effects to remain recognisable.

05

Originality and Ownership

Submit work you created and have the right to enter. Do not copy another designer's work, reuse protected brand artwork without permission, or misrepresent someone else's design as your own.

04 / Creative Freedom

Your idea. Your interpretation.

The purpose of this brief is to establish a shared direction while leaving room for different creative approaches.

There is no single prescribed visual solution.

You are not required to use a particular icon, letterform, shape, colour palette, or layout. You may explore a wordmark, monogram, abstract symbol, geometric composition, or another original approach that communicates the intended brand qualities.

Black and gold may be explored as a colour direction because they feature in the Vanguard Mark website. However, this guide does not make those colours a mandatory condition of entry.

Focus on the strength of your concept, the quality of its execution, and the way its elements work together. The goal is not to produce a variation of the website's existing decoration, but to develop a logo with an identity of its own.

05 / Prepare Your Entry

Before you submit.

Follow these steps to prepare a clear, complete submission.

STEP 01

Develop Your Concept

Create an original logo that responds to the creative brief. Review its clarity, originality, and versatility.

STEP 02

Prepare Your File

STEP 03

Review the Official Rules

Check eligibility, deadlines, judging, ownership, prize conditions, and other applicable requirements before submitting your entry.
