YOUR CHALLENGE Design an original logo for the fictional biscuit brand CRUMBLE & CO.
01 / The Brief

What exactly are you designing?

Every participant starts with the same brief. Your task is to interpret it in your own creative way and produce an original, professional logo.

The CRUMBLE & CO. Logo Challenge

Create an original logo for a fictional biscuit company.

CRUMBLE & CO. is a fictional biscuit and baked-snack brand created specifically for this Vanguard Mark design competition. Imagine the company is preparing to introduce its products to customers and needs a distinctive logo to represent its brand.

Your assignment: Design a logo featuring the name CRUMBLE & CO. The finished design should help establish the brand's identity, attract its intended audience, and make the name easy to recognise and remember.

You may use custom typography, lettering, a biscuit-inspired symbol, an illustration, geometric shapes, or a combination of elements. You have creative freedom to decide how the identity should look.

The objective: Create a memorable, versatile, professionally executed logo that could realistically appear on biscuit packaging, advertisements, supermarket shelves, and digital platforms.

Important: CRUMBLE & CO. is a fictional brand for this creative exercise. It is not presented as an actual company seeking employment or purchasing design services.

02 / Know the Brand

Understand the brand you are designing for.

Think about the product, the customers, and the feeling the brand should create. Use this information to guide your creative decisions.

01 / THE PRODUCT

Biscuits and Baked Snacks

CRUMBLE & CO. is imagined as a biscuit brand offering enjoyable snacks for everyday moments. Your logo should make sense for a food brand and its products.

02 / THE AUDIENCE

Everyday Biscuit Lovers

Consider families, young adults, children, and other biscuit lovers. Develop an identity that appeals to the intended audience without becoming difficult to recognise.

03 / THE PERSONALITY

Warm, Inviting and Memorable

Imagine a brand that feels approachable, trustworthy, and contemporary. Your design can express these qualities through typography, shapes, colour, and visual style.

04 / THE AMBITION

Ready for the Market

Imagine the logo appearing on real product packaging, promotional campaigns, websites, and social media. Aim for an identity that looks polished and adaptable.

03 / Design Expectations

What should your logo achieve?

These are the main considerations for developing a strong entry. Use them to evaluate your work before submission.

01

Use the Correct Brand Name

Your logo must feature the name CRUMBLE & CO. Ensure the wording is correct, legible, and incorporated naturally into the design.

02

Make It Relevant to a Biscuit Brand

Develop an identity that makes sense for biscuits and baked snacks. You may communicate this through lettering, imagery, colour, symbolism, or an original visual concept.

03

Prioritise Originality

Create your own concept rather than copying an existing biscuit brand or another designer's work. Aim for an identity with a recognisable character of its own.

04

Demonstrate Professional Execution

Pay attention to typography, spacing, alignment, proportions, contrast, and visual balance. Every element should serve a purpose in the final design.

05

Consider Packaging and Digital Use

Think about how the logo would appear on a biscuit packet, a shop display, a website, or a mobile screen. It should remain clear and recognisable at different sizes.

06

Submit Work You Have the Right to Enter

Ensure your submission complies with the official competition rules concerning originality, intellectual property, third-party assets, and eligibility.

04 / Creative Freedom

The brief is fixed. Your interpretation is yours.

Every participant designs for the same fictional brand, but each designer can approach the challenge differently.

There is no single prescribed design.

You may develop a wordmark, a custom lettering style, an illustrated biscuit symbol, a distinctive monogram, a geometric concept, or another original visual approach. Choose the direction that best communicates your idea.

You are free to explore colours, typography, composition, and visual styles that suit the brand. You are not required to use the black-and-gold colours of the Vanguard Mark website.

You do not need to draw a literal biscuit. A more abstract concept may work just as well if it creates a meaningful, memorable identity for CRUMBLE & CO.

Focus on the strength of your idea and the quality of its execution. Avoid unnecessary complexity, generic concepts, and visual elements that do not contribute to the brand identity.

05 / Make Your Mark

Think beyond the ordinary.

A successful logo does more than decorate a product. It gives the brand a visual identity that people can recognise, understand, and remember.

01 / ORIGINALITY

Bring a Fresh Perspective

Look for a distinctive idea that gives CRUMBLE & CO. its own identity. Avoid relying on familiar symbols without adding a meaningful creative interpretation.

02 / RELEVANCE

Make the Brand Connection Clear

Ensure your design suits a biscuit brand. Your concept should feel appropriate for the product and its intended audience rather than appearing unrelated to the brief.

03 / CRAFT

Get the Details Right

Refine the typography, shapes, proportions, spacing, and colour relationships. Professional execution can make a creative idea much stronger.

04 / IMPACT

Create a Lasting Impression

Consider whether your logo would stand out on packaging and remain recognisable when viewed quickly or at a smaller size. Make every design decision count.
