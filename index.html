CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

Competition information

Participants should understand how entries are assessed and how the prize is awarded before paying an entry fee.

Original work

Submit work you created or have permission to use. Do not submit logos that infringe another person's rights.

Transparent rules

Eligibility, deadlines, judging criteria, refunds and winner selection must be published before paid entry opens.

Payment security

Entry payments should only be confirmed after the payment provider verifies the transaction.

HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions

How much does an entry cost?

The proposed entry fee is $3. Real payments are not enabled in this demonstration.

How do I know my entry has been received?

A live version should provide an account dashboard and a unique submission reference after the payment and submission have been verified.

How is the winner selected?

The final rules must explain whether selection is based on judging, public voting or another lawful method. The live competition must not accept paid entries until those rules are finalized.

How can I contact support?

Use the official support email published by Vanguard Mark once the address has been configured. No unverified contact details are displayed here.
