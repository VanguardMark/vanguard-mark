PRIVACY & DATA PROTECTION

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Vanguard Mark handles information when you use the website, create an account, enter the competition, or contact support.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information you provide, including your name or display name, email address, account details, uploaded logo designs, submission information, and support messages.

Payment-related information may include transaction references, payment status, amount, currency, and transaction date. Payment providers may independently collect information under their own policies.

Technical information such as browser details, access logs, device information, and security events may be processed when needed to operate and protect the website.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage accounts.
  • Receive and administer logo submissions.
  • Confirm entry payments and investigate payment issues.
  • Provide eligible designs to authorised judges.
  • Verify winners and administer prizes.
  • Respond to support and refund requests.
  • Protect the website from fraud and unauthorised access.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot problems, and meet legal obligations.

We aim to collect and use only information reasonably necessary for these purposes.

3. Lawful Processing

Vanguard Mark aims to collect and process information only where there is an appropriate lawful reason, such as providing a requested service, administering the competition, protecting security, complying with legal obligations, or obtaining consent where required.

4. Sharing Information

We do not intend to make personal information public merely because someone creates an account or submits a design.

Information may be shared with authorised judges, hosting and authentication providers, payment services, technical providers, or prize administrators where reasonably necessary.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, protect security, or defend legal rights.

We aim to limit information shared to what is needed for the relevant purpose.

5. Payments and Third Parties

External payment providers may process payment details under their own terms and privacy policies. Vanguard Mark may receive transaction confirmations and references needed to verify entries.

Third-party websites and services operate under their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information directly to them.

6. Security

We aim to use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

Depending on the systems in use, safeguards may include access controls, authentication, secure connections, restricted administrative access, and security monitoring.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account credentials and report suspected unauthorised access promptly.

7. Data Retention

Information is intended to be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for account administration, competition management, payment reconciliation, support, security, dispute handling, and legal compliance.

When information is no longer needed, we aim to delete it, anonymise it, or securely dispose of it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

8. Your Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where applicable, or raise a complaint with a relevant authority.

Contact support to make a request. We may need to verify your identity, and some information may have to be retained where legally required.

9. Cookies and Technical Storage

The website or its providers may use cookies or browser storage to maintain sessions, remember settings, protect accounts, and support essential features.

If optional analytics or advertising technologies are introduced, their use should be disclosed and consent obtained where required by law.

10. Children's Privacy

The competition is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite children under 18 to enter or pay competition fees.

If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact support so the matter can be reviewed.

11. International Processing

Website providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where a user lives. Appropriate safeguards should be used for international transfers where required by law.

12. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated when services or legal requirements change. The current version will be published on this page. Where required, users will receive notice or be asked for consent.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, access requests, corrections, or concerns, contact support@vanguardmark.com.

Never email passwords, security codes, or full payment-card details.
