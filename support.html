Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How do I enter the logo competition?

Start by creating an account or signing in. Then review the competition rules and follow the available submission instructions.

What is the advertised grand prize?

Vanguard Mark advertises a $50,000 grand prize. Any award remains subject to the official competition terms and verification of available prize funding.

Where can I find the competition deadline?

Visit the homepage and official competition terms for the published competition timeline and applicable submission deadlines.

Can I withdraw a prize through my dashboard?

Prize withdrawal services are not currently active through the dashboard. Do not assume that a prize balance or withdrawal eligibility has been confirmed.

What should I do if a page is not working?

Check your internet connection, reload the page, and confirm that you are using the correct account. If the problem continues, contact the website operator through an officially published support contact.

Still need assistance?

If your question is not answered here, use an official Vanguard Mark support contact if one has been published. Include a description of the issue and the page where you encountered it.

For your security, never send your password, verification codes, or payment card details when requesting assistance.
