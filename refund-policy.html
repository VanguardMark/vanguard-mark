PAYMENTS & REFUNDS

Refund Policy

This policy explains how refunds for Vanguard Mark competition entries are requested and handled.

1. Entry Fee

The entry fee is US$3 per logo submission. Each separate logo submission requires its own fee.

Payment does not guarantee a winning result, shortlist placement, or prize. Any payment-provider fees or currency-conversion charges should be checked before payment.

2. Refund Requests

Participants who wish to request a refund should contact support before the officially announced competition closing deadline.

A request is reviewed individually and is not automatically approved. Support may consider the transaction, submission status, reason for the request, and applicable legal requirements.

Requests should be submitted as early as possible.

3. How to Request a Refund

  1. Email support@vanguardmark.com.
  2. Include your account email and transaction reference.
  3. State the amount paid and explain the reason for your request.
  4. Indicate whether the related logo has already been submitted.
  5. Allow support to review the information and communicate its decision.

Never send passwords, full payment-card numbers, or security codes by email.

4. Processing Approved Refunds

Approved refunds are generally returned through the original payment method where possible and supported by the provider.

Processing times depend on the payment provider, bank, card issuer, or other financial service. Vanguard Mark cannot guarantee the exact date funds will appear in your account.

5. Duplicate Payments or Payment Errors

If you believe you were charged twice, paid the wrong amount, or paid without receiving confirmation, contact support promptly with the relevant transaction references.

Support may check available payment records or consult the payment provider before deciding on the appropriate correction.

6. Competition Cancellation

If the competition is cancelled or a material disruption prevents it from proceeding, Vanguard Mark will communicate the applicable arrangements.

Entry payments will be handled according to the circumstances, the competition terms, payment-provider procedures, and applicable law.

7. Legal Rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove any refund or consumer right that applicable law makes mandatory.

If you believe a refund is legally required, explain the circumstances when contacting support.

8. Unauthorised Payments

If you believe a payment was made without your permission, contact your payment provider promptly and notify Vanguard Mark support.

Your payment provider may require you to complete its own dispute or fraud-reporting process.

9. Contact Support

All refund requests and payment questions should be sent to support@vanguardmark.com.

Include your account email, transaction reference, amount, and a brief explanation so the team can identify the payment.
